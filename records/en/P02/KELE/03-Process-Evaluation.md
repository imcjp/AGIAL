# P02 · KELE: Process evaluation

This evaluation analyzes the teacher support demonstrated during instruction using the established rubric.

## Indicators and scores

Each indicator has a maximum of 15 points. The four equally weighted scores are added, giving a process evaluation maximum of 60 points.

| Indicator | Score |
| --- | ---: |
| P1 Quality of guidance for conceptual understanding | 5/15 |
| P2 Adaptation of support based on learning evidence | 8/15 |
| P3 Support for evidence-based autonomous inquiry | 14/15 |
| P4 Instructional progression and burden management | 8/15 |
| **Process total** | **35/60** |

## P1 Quality of guidance for conceptual understanding: 5/15

Evaluation summary: Locally correct, but missing or inappropriate key reasoning obstructs core understanding.

The local addition relationship and first summation were checkable. However, the side-region explanation switched between different schematics. Round 15 failed to locate the claimed regions in the original six-row diagram or prove that they avoided the collected numbers. The core spatial basis was inappropriate and remained unrepaired, warranting 5 points.

Relevant records: Rounds 5, 7, 9, 12, 14, 15.

## P2 Adaptation of support based on learning evidence: 8/15

Evaluation summary: Basically effective, but with an important mismatch affecting the current need.

The teacher labeled the path and sibling cells as requested and tried explaining the side regions separately. The student wanted to continue using the original six-row diagram, but the teacher subsequently changed to a different schematic, leaving the positioning problem in the original unresolved. The concrete help failed to address the key need, warranting 8 points.

Relevant records: Rounds 3, 4, 5, 7, 10, 12, 13, 14, 15.

## P3 Support for evidence-based autonomous inquiry: 14/15

Evaluation summary: Invitations become actual participation in evidence-based judgment; the teacher responds openly and rigorously to contributions concerning questions, criteria, or evidence and allows them to influence progression.

The student actually checked numbers, pointed out cell conflicts, and requested retention of the original diagram. The teacher accepted these challenges and adjusted, supporting 14 points. The basis for judging the regions remained unstable; continued questioning alone cannot be regarded as complete inquiry.

Relevant records: Rounds 1, 2, 3, 4, 9, 12, 14, 15.

## P4 Instructional progression and burden management: 8/15

Evaluation summary: The task can be completed, but there is substantive redundancy, discontinuity, or avoidable detouring.

Labels aided local searching, but changing diagrams and unnecessary reconfirmation required repeated locating. Round 14 restated a relationship the student had already calculated, and round 15 changed diagrams again, increasing switching costs and warranting 8 points.

Relevant records: Rounds 3, 5, 7, 10, 11, 12, 13, 14, 15.

## Combined process and experience evaluation

The process evaluation is 35/60 and the learning experience score is 20/40; adding them gives a total of **55/100**.
