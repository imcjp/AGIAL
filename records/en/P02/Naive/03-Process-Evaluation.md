# P02 · Naive: Process evaluation

This evaluation analyzes the teacher support demonstrated during instruction using the established rubric.

## Indicators and scores

Each indicator has a maximum of 15 points. The four equally weighted scores are added, giving a process evaluation maximum of 60 points.

| Indicator | Score |
| --- | ---: |
| P1 Quality of guidance for conceptual understanding | 8/15 |
| P2 Adaptation of support based on learning evidence | 8/15 |
| P3 Support for evidence-based autonomous inquiry | 14/15 |
| P4 Instructional progression and burden management | 8/15 |
| **Process total** | **38/60** |

## P1 Quality of guidance for conceptual understanding: 8/15

Evaluation summary: The main explanations are effective, but substantive gaps affect current understanding.

Round 7 corrected the claim that the first accumulation already produced a contradiction, explaining that equality only fixed the small numbers in the top and sibling cells. Later responses also distinguished path numbers from summands. But rounds 8 and 9 never explained why the side triangle avoided all summands; the numerical comparison depended on an unestablished positional condition. Local relationships were effective, but the core proof still contained a substantive gap, warranting 8 points.

Relevant records: Rounds 3, 4, 5, 6, 7, 8, 9.

## P2 Adaptation of support based on learning evidence: 8/15

Evaluation summary: Basically effective, but with an important mismatch affecting the current need.

The teacher could switch to small triangles and individually labeled cells after the student's requests and accepted a narrower discussion scope. But the student repeatedly asked to see the side regions clearly, while later responses still mainly explained summation or supplied examples unable to display the target region. The most needed spatial support was not delivered, warranting 8 points.

Relevant records: Rounds 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7, 8, 9.

## P3 Support for evidence-based autonomous inquiry: 14/15

Evaluation summary: Invitations become actual participation in evidence-based judgment; the teacher responds openly and rigorously to contributions concerning questions, criteria, or evidence and allows them to influence progression.

The student's trial arrangements, distinction between summands, and challenges about diagram positions all received responses and changed the teacher's subsequent explanations. Participation included actual evidence checking, supporting 14 points. The grounds needed to judge the side regions remained incomplete throughout, preventing a complete inquiry process that could sustain further testing.

Relevant records: Rounds 2, 4, 5, 6, 7, 9.

## P4 Instructional progression and burden management: 8/15

Evaluation summary: The task can be completed, but there is substantive redundancy, discontinuity, or avoidable detouring.

Small triangles and single-step equations provided usable entry points, but the student had to repeatedly distinguish the path from the summands, check positions in the diagrams, and ask again about side regions. In the later part, key connections still had to be supplied independently. These exchanges had identifiable content-related causes that shorter responses could not offset, warranting 8 points.

Relevant records: Rounds 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 7, 8, 9.

## Combined process and experience evaluation

The process evaluation is 38/60 and the learning experience score is 21/40; adding them gives a total of **59/100**.
