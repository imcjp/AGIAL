# P02 · AGIAL: Process evaluation

This evaluation analyzes the teacher support demonstrated during instruction using the established rubric.

## Indicators and scores

Each indicator has a maximum of 15 points. The four equally weighted scores are added, giving a process evaluation maximum of 60 points.

| Indicator | Score |
| --- | ---: |
| P1 Quality of guidance for conceptual understanding | 8/15 |
| P2 Adaptation of support based on learning evidence | 14/15 |
| P3 Support for evidence-based autonomous inquiry | 14/15 |
| P4 Instructional progression and burden management | 8/15 |
| **Process total** | **44/60** |

## P1 Quality of guidance for conceptual understanding: 8/15

Evaluation summary: The main explanations are effective, but substantive gaps affect current understanding.

The small triangle supported the relationship that the larger child equals the upper cell plus its sibling, and the teacher also explained the lower bound for a sum of distinct positive integers and the implications of equality. When moving to 2018 rows in round 5, the reasoning connecting the side triangle with the second accumulation was not developed; round 6 confirmed only a single-step equation. The core conclusion still lacked necessary explanation, warranting 8 points.

Relevant records: Rounds 4, 5, 6.

## P2 Adaptation of support based on learning evidence: 14/15

Evaluation summary: Precise alignment is supported by specific learning evidence and actual interaction; the teacher changes or retains appropriate help in response to visible needs. Additional rounds are not required.

The student requested one problem at a time. The teacher accordingly fixed one bottom-row number, checked the middle before the top, and returned to a single-step relationship after providing too much information. The student's completion of the small triangle and transfer of the equation support 14 points. The local help was not yet coherently connected back to the complete judgment about 2018 rows, so 15 points are not awarded.

Relevant records: Rounds 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6.

## P3 Support for evidence-based autonomous inquiry: 14/15

Evaluation summary: Invitations become actual participation in evidence-based judgment; the teacher responds openly and rigorously to contributions concerning questions, criteria, or evidence and allows them to influence progression.

The student actually selected numbers, calculated, and checked the non-repetition condition. The teacher adjusted scaffolding based on failed attempts and used the successful construction to discuss the scope of the evidence. The inquiry had genuine substance, warranting 14 points; the teacher nevertheless passed over the key examination needed for the nonexistence conclusion, leaving the chain of judgment incomplete.

Relevant records: Rounds 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6.

## P4 Instructional progression and burden management: 8/15

Evaluation summary: The task can be completed, but there is substantive redundancy, discontinuity, or avoidable detouring.

Checking the middle and top separately did reduce the local burden. But round 5 introduced the path, minimum sum, equality, and conclusion about the large triangle together, immediately prompting the student to request a return to one equation; the student still had to connect multiple relationships independently at the end. The exchange contained substantive discontinuity, warranting 8 points.

Relevant records: Rounds 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6.

## Combined process and experience evaluation

The process evaluation is 44/60 and the learning experience score is 27/40; adding them gives a total of **71/100**.
