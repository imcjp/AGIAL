# P05 · AGIAL:Process evaluation

This evaluation analyzes the teacher support demonstrated during instruction using the established rubric.

## Indicators and scores

Each indicator has a maximum of 15 points. The four equally weighted indicators are added to yield a process evaluation maximum of 60 points.

| Indicator | Score |
| --- | ---: |
| P1 Quality of guidance for conceptual understanding | 15/15 |
| P2 Adaptation of support based on learning evidence | 15/15 |
| P3 Support for evidence-based autonomous inquiry | 15/15 |
| P4 Instructional progression and burden management | 15/15 |
| **Process total** | **60/60** |

## P1 Quality of guidance for conceptual understanding:15/15

Evaluation summary: Establishes a reasoned, bounded, reusable basis for understanding, supported across the full observation window rather than merely adding summary slogans.

The teacher connects local addition to the ordering-based lower bound and equality case, explains the column ranges of the common parent and sibling cells, and compares them with the side-region boundaries. Step counts, complete subtriangles, and the distinction between failure to exclude and existence are all explained, establishing a bounded basis for judgment and warranting 15 points.

Relevant records: Rounds 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6.

## P2 Adaptation of support based on learning evidence:15/15

Evaluation summary: Coherently reconnects local help to the current task of complete understanding, taking prior help into account rather than using round counts or scaffold withdrawal to raise the rating.

The student wants to examine regions first while retaining responsibility for deriving magnitudes. The teacher supplies a position diagram and explains its applicability, directly develops the general reasoning when needed, and subsequently leaves the numerical comparison to the student. The help connects to the work the student wishes to undertake, warranting 15 points.

Relevant records: Rounds 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6.

## P3 Support for evidence-based autonomous inquiry:15/15

Evaluation summary: Relevant judgments, examination of evidence, and revision of claims form coherent inquiry; the language supports participation while maintaining autonomy.

The student actually constructs examples, derives lower bounds, and checks step counts and edge cases. The teacher builds on these contributions and explains which conclusions are supported and which remain to be proved. The division between explanation and autonomous judgment is clear, warranting 15 points.

Relevant records: Rounds 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6.

## P4 Instructional progression and burden management:15/15

Evaluation summary: Explanations and activities are coherent throughout the complete task: complex points are explained thoroughly, clear points are not belabored, and concision neither omits necessary content nor transfers the burden.

The specific diagram addresses positions only, the general expression explains arbitrary paths, and calculations build on the student’s existing results. The later section supplements only step counts and the limits of the conclusion, without repeating the whole proof. Complex points are developed and clear points are brought to a close, warranting 15 points.

Relevant records: Rounds 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6.

## Combined process and experience evaluation

Process evaluation: 60/60; learning experience: 35/40. Adding the two yields a total score of **95/100**.
