# P05 · Naive:Process evaluation

This evaluation analyzes the teacher support demonstrated during instruction using the established rubric.

## Indicators and scores

Each indicator has a maximum of 15 points. The four equally weighted indicators are added to yield a process evaluation maximum of 60 points.

| Indicator | Score |

| P2 Adaptation of support based on learning evidence | 14/15 |

## P1 Quality of guidance for conceptual understanding:14/15

Evaluation summary: Actual interaction connects relevant concepts, reasoning, and conditions, clarifying key points of disconnection.

Problems arise successively in the initial small triangle and the descendant range. The teacher corrects them in Rounds 2 and 6, then explains the link between side regions and summation. The interaction clarifies key disconnections, warranting 14 points. However, the examples, positional reasoning, and general conclusion are not consistently aligned throughout, leaving insufficient support for the highest category.

Relevant records: Rounds 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6.

## P2 Adaptation of support based on learning evidence:14/15

Evaluation summary: Precise alignment is supported by specific learning evidence and actual interaction; the teacher changes or retains appropriate help according to visible needs. Additional rounds are not required.

The teacher changes the example and corrects the descendant range in response to the student’s actual challenges, providing specific help that addresses immediate problems. However, two key representations require the student to point out conflicts first, so the connection between local help and the complete task is insufficiently stable, warranting 14 points.

Relevant records: Rounds 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6.

## P3 Support for evidence-based autonomous inquiry:14/15

Evaluation summary: Invitations lead to actual, evidence-based participation in judgment; the teacher responds openly and rigorously to contributions concerning questions, criteria, or evidence and allows them to influence progression.

The student’s constructions, range checks, and counterexamples are taken up and change subsequent explanations. The teacher supports actual judgment, but several invitations are based on materials still needing correction, limiting continuity in the examination of evidence and warranting 14 points.

Relevant records: Rounds 2, 3, 4, 5, 6.

## P4 Instructional progression and burden management:8/15

Evaluation summary: The task can be completed, but there is substantive redundancy, discontinuity, or avoidable detouring.

The student must first verify the example and then investigate the descendant range, while the same proof is repeatedly patched across different representations. The usable explanation later does not remove this additional work, warranting 8 points.

Relevant records: Rounds 1, 2, 4, 5, 6.

## Combined process and experience evaluation

Process evaluation: 50/60; learning experience: 31/40. Adding the two yields a total score of **81/100**.
