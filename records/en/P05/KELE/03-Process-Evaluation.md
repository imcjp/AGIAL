## P1 Quality of guidance for conceptual understanding:14/15

Evaluation summary: Actual interaction connects relevant concepts, reasoning, and conditions, clarifying key points of disconnection.

The reasoning in Round 6 for excluding sibling cells using fifth-row descendants is insufficient. In Round 7, the teacher acknowledges this and switches to bottom-row descendants, subsequently connecting the second summation and the limits of the argument when the numerical upper bound is removed. The main concepts are connected, but why both child cells reach the final pair remains rather general, warranting 14 points.

Relevant records: Rounds 3, 4, 5, 6, 7, 8.

## P2 Adaptation of support based on learning evidence:14/15

Evaluation summary: Precise alignment is supported by specific learning evidence and actual interaction; the teacher changes or retains appropriate help according to visible needs. Additional rounds are not required.

The teacher provides a six-row indexed diagram as requested and changes the spatial explanation in response to a counterexample. The student can subsequently calculate and check conditions using it. Specific feedback supports the local help, but earlier fragmented rewrites and position explanations have not yet achieved smooth coherence, warranting 14 points.

Relevant records: Rounds 1, 3, 6, 7, 8.

## P3 Support for evidence-based autonomous inquiry:15/15

Evaluation summary: Relevant judgments, examination of evidence, and revision of claims form coherent inquiry; the language supports participation while maintaining autonomy.

The student uses a counterexample to revise the positional criterion, then independently compares the second lower bound and proposes a variation removing the numerical upper bound. The teacher explicitly adopts these contributions, checks which reasons fail in the variation, and limits the conclusion that can be drawn. Inquiry is coherent, warranting 15 points.

Relevant records: Rounds 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7, 8.

## P4 Instructional progression and burden management:8/15

Evaluation summary: The task can be completed, but there is substantive redundancy, discontinuity, or avoidable detouring.

The student first asks about the purpose of a one-step rewrite, then needs to refute the exclusion argument based on fifth-row descendants. The indexed diagram and corrected explanation are useful, but these avoidable rechecks constitute a substantive burden, warranting 8 points.

Relevant records: Rounds 3, 4, 5, 6, 7, 8.

## Combined process and experience evaluation

Process evaluation: 51/60; learning experience: 33/40. Adding the two yields a total score of **84/100**.
