## P1 Quality of guidance for conceptual understanding: 8/15

Evaluation summary: The summation and descendant arguments have substantive content, but the path diagram supplied for checking retains a key contradiction.

The teacher explained path accumulation, equality, and the second lower bound, and Round 12 also supplied an approach using descendant ranges to exclude sibling cells. However, the P in row 4, column 2 of the Round 11 diagram should have its children in row 5, columns 2, 3, while the teacher described P in column 3 and a in column 4 as its children. The bottom-row children of P in row 5, column 3 should likewise occupy columns 3, 4 rather than columns 4, 5 as shown. This set of parent-child relationships was not corrected later, and the student treated the diagram as verified evidence. The general argument and concrete diagram still could not be used consistently, constituting a substantive gap affecting current spatial understanding. The score is 8.

Relevant records: Rounds 6, 7, 8, 9, 10, 11, 12, 13, 14.

## P2 Adaptation of support based on learning evidence: 14/15

Evaluation summary: Precise alignment is supported by specific learning evidence and actual interaction; the teacher changes or retains suitable assistance according to visible needs, without requiring additional rounds.

In Round 6, the teacher demonstrated accumulation using the student's own three-row triangle, then added a diagram and explained descendant ranges in response to positional questions; feedback supported these adjustments. In Round 5, however, a request for demonstration was answered with another question about a relationship already understood, showing early misalignment. The score is 14 rather than 15.

Relevant records: Rounds 5, 6, 7, 11, 12, 13.

## P3 Support for evidence-based autonomous inquiry: 14/15

Evaluation summary: The student's substantive judgments are taken up, but one key check receives no reliable feedback.

The teacher let the student judge construction, the number of summands, equality, and the second lower bound. After the student noted that “excluding the path does not mean excluding sibling cells,” the teacher specifically added the descendant argument, so substantive contributions did change progression. Round 11 also invited row-by-row diagram checking, but when the student reported correct sibling-cell labels in Round 12, the teacher did not verify the parent-child correspondences or complete a revision accordingly. There was ample substantive participation in judgment, but examination of evidence and revision were not coherently implemented across the complete observation window. The score is 14.

Relevant records: Rounds 2, 3, 4, 6, 7, 8, 9, 10, 11, 12, 13, 14.

## P4 Instructional progression and burden management: 8/15

Evaluation summary: The task can be completed, but substantive redundancy, leaps, or avoidable detours remain.

Round 5 repeated questions about a rule the student already knew, and in Round 6 the student explicitly reported going in circles. The wording about the final pair also needed separate clarification. Although later segmented explanations were effective, the whole exchange contained avoidable back-and-forth. The score is 8.

Relevant records: Rounds 4, 5, 6, 7, 10, 11, 12, 13, 14.

## Combined process and experience evaluation

The process evaluation is 44/60 and the learning experience score is 29/40. Adding them gives a total of **73/100**.
