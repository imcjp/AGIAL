# P07 · AGIAL: Process evaluation

This evaluation analyzes the teacher support demonstrated during teaching using the established rubric.

## Indicators and scores

Each indicator has a maximum of 15 points. The four indicators are added with equal weight, giving a maximum process evaluation score of 60 points.

| Indicator | Score |
| --- | ---: |
| P1 Quality of guidance for conceptual understanding | 15/15 |
| P2 Adaptation of support based on learning evidence | 15/15 |
| P3 Support for evidence-based autonomous inquiry | 15/15 |
| P4 Instructional progression and burden management | 15/15 |
| **Process total** | **60/60** |

## P1 Quality of guidance for conceptual understanding: 15/15

Evaluation summary: Establishes a reasoned, bounded, and reusable basis for understanding, supported across the complete observation window rather than merely adding a concluding slogan.

The teacher explicitly distinguished intermediate path values from summands, used a variation allowing repeats to explain the conditions for the lower bound, and then explained separation from the side regions through an always-left path and general column indices. Both the second lower bound and the extraction of a complete subtriangle were justified. The score is 15.

Relevant records: Rounds 2, 3, 5, 6, 7.

## P2 Adaptation of support based on learning evidence: 15/15

Evaluation summary: Connects local assistance coherently back to the current complete task of understanding, takes prior assistance into account, and does not rely on the number of rounds or withdrawal of scaffolding to raise the rating.

When the student requested one direction at a time, the teacher first explained addition, then offered a variation in the conditions and a small diagram of the side regions. When asked about always going left, the teacher addressed that case before explaining arbitrary paths. The assistance connected with the student's current needs in calculation, positioning, and generalization. The score is 15.

Relevant records: Rounds 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7.

## P3 Support for evidence-based autonomous inquiry: 15/15

Evaluation summary: Relevant judgments, examination of evidence, and revision of claims form a coherent inquiry; the language supports participation while maintaining autonomy.

The student actually tested the condition concerning repeats and calculated the size of the side region and the second lower bound. The teacher verified this reasoning and preserved room to ask for help. At the end, the teacher distinguished the student's ability to connect the numerical contradiction from the part still requiring the column explanation. The origins of contributions and assistance were clear. The score is 15.

Relevant records: Rounds 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7.

## P4 Instructional progression and burden management: 15/15

Evaluation summary: Explanations and activities are coherent throughout the complete task; complex points are explained thoroughly and clear points are not belabored, with concision that neither omits content nor transfers the burden to the learner.

The concrete diagram resolved confusion first, and the general formulas were developed only after the student asked about arbitrary paths. At the end, the teacher reused the completed numerical calculations and added only the conditions for truncation, without repeating the entire proof. The score is 15.

Relevant records: Rounds 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7.

## Combined process and experience evaluation

The process evaluation is 60/60 and the learning experience score is 31/40. Adding them gives a total of **91/100**.
