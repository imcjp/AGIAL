# P07 · KELE: Process evaluation

This evaluation analyzes the teacher support demonstrated during teaching using the established rubric.

## Indicators and scores

Each indicator has a maximum of 15 points. The four indicators are added with equal weight, giving a maximum process evaluation score of 60 points.

| Indicator | Score |
| --- | ---: |
| P1 Quality of guidance for conceptual understanding | 14/15 |
| P2 Adaptation of support based on learning evidence | 14/15 |
| P3 Support for evidence-based autonomous inquiry | 14/15 |
| P4 Instructional progression and burden management | 8/15 |
| **Process total** | **50/60** |

## P1 Quality of guidance for conceptual understanding: 14/15

Evaluation summary: Connects relevant concepts, reasons, and conditions through actual interaction, clarifying key breaks in understanding.

Calculations of side-region width, diagrams, and row numbers successively required correction. The coordinate relationships supplied later could connect with the general argument. Key knowledge was clarified, but the same relationship was insufficiently stable across representations. The score is 14.

Relevant records: Rounds 2, 3, 5, 6, 7, 8, 9.

## P2 Adaptation of support based on learning evidence: 14/15

Evaluation summary: Precise alignment is supported by specific learning evidence and actual interaction; the teacher changes or retains suitable assistance according to visible needs, without requiring additional rounds.

The teacher adjusted explanations in response to questions about diagrams and positions, and the later general formulas provided verifiable grounds. Several earlier adjustments generated new questions; local assistance was useful, but coordination across the complete task remained limited. The score is 14.

Relevant records: Rounds 1, 4, 7, 8, 9.

## P3 Support for evidence-based autonomous inquiry: 14/15

Evaluation summary: Invitations become evidence-based participation in actual judgment; the teacher openly and rigorously takes up contributions concerning questions, criteria, or evidence and allows them to influence progression.

The student offered numerical and positional counterexamples, which the teacher accepted and used to make corrections; substantive contributions influenced the discussion. Some objects underlying later tasks still required prior correction. The score is 14.

Relevant records: Rounds 1, 2, 3, 6, 7, 8, 9.

## P4 Instructional progression and burden management: 8/15

Evaluation summary: The task can be completed, but substantive redundancy, leaps, or avoidable detours remain.

Equations, black-and-white diagrams, row numbers, and side-region ranges changed several times, requiring the student to return repeatedly to the original diagram. Clear coordinates later do not erase this reworking. The score is 8.

Relevant records: Rounds 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7, 8, 9.

## Combined process and experience evaluation

The process evaluation is 50/60 and the learning experience score is 28/40. Adding them gives a total of **78/100**.
