## P1 Quality of guidance for conceptual understanding:14/15

Evaluation summary: Actual interaction connects relevant concepts, reasoning, and conditions, clarifying key points of disconnection.

Local accumulation, distinctness, and equality are clearly explained. In Round 4, the row indices and ranges of the eight-row example are inconsistent. In Round 5, the teacher accepts the student’s correction but does not fully redraw the incorrect schematic. The later section clarifies the ordering-based lower bound and the limits of existence claims, supporting 14 points. However, correspondence among the diagram, coordinates, and general explanation does not meet the highest-category requirements.

Relevant records: Rounds 2, 3, 4, 5, 6.

## P2 Adaptation of support based on learning evidence:14/15

Evaluation summary: Precise alignment is supported by specific learning evidence and actual interaction; the teacher changes or retains appropriate help according to visible needs. Additional rounds are not required.

The teacher first retains the student’s independent attempts, then explains the identities of summands and spatial positions. The student specifically identifies the benefit of coordinates, supporting 14 points. However, the original schematic increases confusion, so coordination of the complete help remains limited.

Relevant records: Rounds 2, 3, 4, 5, 6.

## P3 Support for evidence-based autonomous inquiry:15/15

Evaluation summary: Relevant judgments, examination of evidence, and revision of claims form coherent inquiry; the language supports participation while maintaining autonomy.

The student points out that equality is not yet a contradiction, checks coordinates, and uses a three-row example to distinguish a lower bound within the limit from the existence of a construction. The teacher checks and retains these judgments and further explains the proof’s applicability, forming coherent examination of evidence and warranting 15 points.

Relevant records: Rounds 1, 2, 3, 5, 6.

## P4 Instructional progression and burden management:8/15

Evaluation summary: The task can be completed, but there is substantive redundancy, discontinuity, or avoidable detouring.

The row indices, ranges, and schematic in Round 4 are inconsistent, requiring the student to reorganize their correspondences independently. The student later explicitly states that the diagram caused confusion. Brief confirmation and the final organization of applicability limits are effective, but cannot offset this rework, warranting 8 points.

Relevant records: Rounds 2, 3, 4, 5, 6.

## Combined process and experience evaluation

Process evaluation: 51/60; learning experience: 33/40. Adding the two yields a total score of **84/100**.
