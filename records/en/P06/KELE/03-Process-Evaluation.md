# P06 · KELE:Process evaluation

This evaluation analyzes the teacher support demonstrated during instruction using the established rubric.

## Indicators and scores

Each indicator has a maximum of 15 points. The four equally weighted indicators are added to yield a process evaluation maximum of 60 points.

| Indicator | Score |
| --- | ---: |
| P1 Quality of guidance for conceptual understanding | 15/15 |
| P2 Adaptation of support based on learning evidence | 15/15 |
| P3 Support for evidence-based autonomous inquiry | 15/15 |
| P4 Instructional progression and burden management | 8/15 |
| **Process total** | **53/60** |

## P1 Quality of guidance for conceptual understanding:15/15

Evaluation summary: Establishes a reasoned, bounded, reusable basis for understanding, supported across the full observation window rather than merely adding summary slogans.

The teacher explains positivity, distinctness, and equality, and gives a complete positional argument using the common parent and boundaries within the same row. Round 6 specifies that a single cell also counts as a one-row side triangle. The check in Round 8 completes the lower-bound comparison with a small number of increments. Conditions and reasoning are reusable, warranting 15 points.

Relevant records: Rounds 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7, 8.

## P2 Adaptation of support based on learning evidence:15/15

Evaluation summary: Coherently reconnects local help to the current task of complete understanding, taking prior help into account rather than using round counts or scaffold withdrawal to raise the rating.

The teacher takes up the student’s question about partial sums possibly skipping small numbers and turns to side regions. When the student needs positional reasoning, coordinates are fully developed; when the student proposes a rough comparison, that approach is retained. Local help is ultimately coordinated with the complete contradiction, warranting 15 points.

Relevant records: Rounds 1, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7, 8.

## P3 Support for evidence-based autonomous inquiry:15/15

Evaluation summary: Relevant judgments, examination of evidence, and revision of claims form coherent inquiry; the language supports participation while maintaining autonomy.

The student checks positivity, derives the equality case, examines regions, and proposes a rough comparison. The teacher explains its purpose and checks its sufficiency. The student’s judgments are used continuously, and the proof can ultimately be organized using the supplied lemma, warranting 15 points.

Relevant records: Rounds 1, 2, 4, 5, 6, 7, 8.

## P4 Instructional progression and burden management:8/15

Evaluation summary: The task can be completed, but there is substantive redundancy, discontinuity, or avoidable detouring.

In Round 5, the teacher asks the student to find a small triangle without explaining that this example leaves only single-cell side triangles. Consequently, the student checks nonexistent three-cell regions one by one. The later acknowledgment and clarification cannot remove the ineffective search that has already occurred, warranting 8 points.

Relevant records: Rounds 3, 4, 5, 6, 7, 8.

## Combined process and experience evaluation

Process evaluation: 53/60; learning experience: 32/40. Adding the two yields a total score of **85/100**.
