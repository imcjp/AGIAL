# P06 · StratL:Process evaluation

This evaluation analyzes the teacher support demonstrated during instruction using the established rubric.

## Indicators and scores

Each indicator has a maximum of 15 points. The four equally weighted indicators are added to yield a process evaluation maximum of 60 points.

| Indicator | Score |
| --- | ---: |
| P1 Quality of guidance for conceptual understanding | 15/15 |
| P2 Adaptation of support based on learning evidence | 8/15 |
| P3 Support for evidence-based autonomous inquiry | 14/15 |
| P4 Instructional progression and burden management | 8/15 |
| **Process total** | **45/60** |

## P1 Quality of guidance for conceptual understanding:15/15

Evaluation summary: Establishes a reasoned, bounded, reusable basis for understanding, supported across the full observation window rather than merely adding summary slogans.

The later section provides ranges for the common parent and child cells, compares side-region boundaries in the same row, then applies distinctness to the second lower bound. The roles of positions, numerical values, and equality are connected and can be used to check different paths, warranting 15 points.

Relevant records: Rounds 4, 5, 6, 9, 10.

## P2 Adaptation of support based on learning evidence:8/15

Evaluation summary: Basically effective, but important mismatches affect current needs.

After the student completes the local relationships and requests the positions of the side regions, the teacher returns to questioning the earlier relationships. The eventual general explanation is useful, but repeatedly returning to already understood content does not meet the need at that time, warranting 8 points.

Relevant records: Rounds 3, 4, 7, 8, 9, 10.

## P3 Support for evidence-based autonomous inquiry:14/15

Evaluation summary: Invitations lead to actual, evidence-based participation in judgment; the teacher responds openly and rigorously to contributions concerning questions, criteria, or evidence and allows them to influence progression.

The teacher takes up the student’s numerical reasoning and positional questions, and participation in judgment has actual substance, warranting 14 points. Repeated questioning of completed portions means subsequent inquiry does not consistently advance along the questions currently being raised by the student.

Relevant records: Rounds 2, 4, 5, 6, 7, 8, 9, 10.

## P4 Instructional progression and burden management:8/15

Evaluation summary: The task can be completed, but there is substantive redundancy, discontinuity, or avoidable detouring.

Local relationships are repeatedly confirmed, requiring the student to explain again that the sticking point has shifted to spatial issues. The final complete explanation reduces later work connecting the pieces, but the earlier detours still constitute a substantive burden, warranting 8 points.

Relevant records: Rounds 3, 5, 6, 7, 8, 9, 10.

## Combined process and experience evaluation

Process evaluation: 45/60; learning experience: 26/40. Adding the two yields a total score of **71/100**.
