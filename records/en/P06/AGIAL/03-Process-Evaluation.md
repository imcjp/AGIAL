## P1 Quality of guidance for conceptual understanding:15/15

Evaluation summary: Establishes a reasoned, bounded, reusable basis for understanding, supported across the full observation window rather than merely adding summary slogans.

The teacher explains the addition relationship for the larger child cell, the minimum sum of distinct numbers, and the equality case, then provides column-based reasoning for separation of the side regions. Both the second summation and the conditions for extracting a complete subtriangle are checked. Reasoning and applicability limits are complete, warranting 15 points.

Relevant records: Rounds 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6.

## P2 Adaptation of support based on learning evidence:15/15

Evaluation summary: Coherently reconnects local help to the current task of complete understanding, taking prior help into account rather than using round counts or scaffold withdrawal to raise the rating.

Starting with the student’s small-triangle attempts, the teacher adjusts among local calculations, magnitude constraints, and the position proof according to actual requests for help. General reasoning is directly supplied when needed, completed calculations are retained, and the help reconnects to the original problem, warranting 15 points.

Relevant records: Rounds 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6.

## P3 Support for evidence-based autonomous inquiry:15/15

Evaluation summary: Relevant judgments, examination of evidence, and revision of claims form coherent inquiry; the language supports participation while maintaining autonomy.

The teacher takes up relationships that remain valid in the student’s unsuccessful attempt and retains the student’s judgments about equality and lower bounds. The student checks the three-cell condition, row indices, and empty side regions. Relevant evidence affects subsequent explanations, forming coherent inquiry and warranting 15 points.

Relevant records: Rounds 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6.

## P4 Instructional progression and burden management:15/15

Evaluation summary: Explanations and activities are coherent throughout the complete task: complex points are explained thoroughly, clear points are not belabored, and concision neither omits necessary content nor transfers the burden.

Each addition addresses the current gap: small triangles for local relationships, column indices for arbitrary paths, and only the method of obtaining a complete subtriangle for edge cases. The student need not redo established judgments, and necessary explanations are not omitted, warranting 15 points.

Relevant records: Rounds 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6.
