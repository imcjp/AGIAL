Refer to what actually happened to explain the help or shortcomings that most influenced your scores, and state the one thing you most want to retain or change. What helped most was letting me try a three-row triangle first and, when I got stuck, giving only the clue to put 1 in the middle of the bottom row, so I could still complete the calculations myself.
