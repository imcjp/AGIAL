# P03 · StratL: Process evaluation

This evaluation analyzes the teacher support demonstrated during instruction using the established rubric.

## Indicators and scores

Each indicator has a maximum of 15 points. The four equally weighted scores are added, giving a process evaluation maximum of 60 points.

| Indicator | Score |
| --- | ---: |
| P1 Quality of guidance for conceptual understanding | 15/15 |
| P2 Adaptation of support based on learning evidence | 8/15 |
| P3 Support for evidence-based autonomous inquiry | 14/15 |
| P4 Instructional progression and burden management | 8/15 |
| **Process total** | **45/60** |

## P1 Quality of guidance for conceptual understanding: 15/15

Evaluation summary: Understanding rests on reasoned, bounded, reusable grounds; support must span the full interaction window rather than merely adding a concluding slogan.

The later responses explained the shared parent's reachable range, sibling column numbers, and boundaries of same-row side regions, connected them to the second summation, and preserved the distinction between a small example and a general conclusion. The reasons and conditions of applicability formed reusable grounds for judgment, warranting 15 points.

Relevant records: Rounds 2, 4, 5, 6, 7, 9, 10, 11.

## P2 Adaptation of support based on learning evidence: 8/15

Evaluation summary: Basically effective, but with an important mismatch affecting the current need.

The student repeatedly and explicitly requested concrete quantities, examples, and positions, but the teacher still asked for continued searching or judgment, supplying the needed explanation only later. The eventual explanation was useful, but the earlier repeated mismatches directly affected the current task, warranting 8 points.

Relevant records: Rounds 3, 4, 5, 6, 8, 9, 10, 11.

## P3 Support for evidence-based autonomous inquiry: 14/15

Evaluation summary: Invitations become actual participation in evidence-based judgment; the teacher responds openly and rigorously to contributions concerning questions, criteria, or evidence and allows them to influence progression.

The teacher recognized the student's quantitative reasoning and positional challenges and used them to advance the proof; actual participation supports 14 points. Several requests for help were turned back into problems to solve, and some judgments were returned to the student without the necessary help, falling short of 15 points.

Relevant records: Rounds 2, 3, 4, 5, 7, 8, 9, 10, 11.

## P4 Instructional progression and burden management: 8/15

Evaluation summary: The task can be completed, but there is substantive redundancy, discontinuity, or avoidable detouring.

The final positional proof reduced the need to keep guessing from diagrams, but earlier repeated questioning about already explicit obstacles required the student to explain the needed help several times. Ineffective back-and-forth was concretely evident, warranting 8 points.

Relevant records: Rounds 3, 4, 5, 8, 9, 10, 11.

## Combined process and experience evaluation

The process evaluation is 45/60 and the learning experience score is 29/40; adding them gives a total of **74/100**.
