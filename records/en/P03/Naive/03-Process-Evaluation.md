# P03 · Naive: Process evaluation

This evaluation analyzes the teacher support demonstrated during instruction using the established rubric.

## Indicators and scores

Each indicator has a maximum of 15 points. The four equally weighted scores are added, giving a process evaluation maximum of 60 points.

| Indicator | Score |
| --- | ---: |
| P1 Quality of guidance for conceptual understanding | 14/15 |
| P2 Adaptation of support based on learning evidence | 14/15 |
| P3 Support for evidence-based autonomous inquiry | 14/15 |
| P4 Instructional progression and burden management | 8/15 |
| **Process total** | **50/60** |

## P1 Quality of guidance for conceptual understanding: 14/15

Evaluation summary: Actual interaction connects relevant concepts, reasons, and conditions and clarifies key gaps.

Round 2 corrected the claim that left-right symmetry could move the maximum to a corner. Later responses explained equality, occupation of the small numbers, and the side-triangle lower bound. The omitted cell label in the side-region diagram was also supplied in round 6. The interaction clarified key connections, supporting 14 points; the account of the sibling's descendant range remained rather general, and the complete boundary justification did not yet meet the highest-level requirement.

Relevant records: Rounds 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6.

## P2 Adaptation of support based on learning evidence: 14/15

Evaluation summary: Precise alignment is supported by specific learning evidence and actual interaction; the teacher changes or retains appropriate help in response to visible needs. Additional rounds are not required.

The student requested an explanation of equality before positions, and the teacher narrowed the scope accordingly, then used descendant intervals and a completed five-row diagram. Specific feedback showed that these forms of help resolved the questions at the time, warranting 14 points. Diagrams and text were not consistently coordinated, and local help remained insufficient for 15 points.

Relevant records: Rounds 3, 4, 5, 6.

## P3 Support for evidence-based autonomous inquiry: 14/15

Evaluation summary: Invitations become actual participation in evidence-based judgment; the teacher responds openly and rigorously to contributions concerning questions, criteria, or evidence and allows them to influence progression.

The teacher took up substantive student contributions including construction, distinguishing the path from summands, completing regional labels, and checking edges. Some key connections were still supplied by the student and then received only general confirmation; the teacher's verification of evidence was incomplete. The score is 14 points.

Relevant records: Rounds 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6.

## P4 Instructional progression and burden management: 8/15

Evaluation summary: The task can be completed, but there is substantive redundancy, discontinuity, or avoidable detouring.

Symmetry, the identity of summands, and omitted side-region labels required successive clarification, leading the student to recheck examples already in use. Although completing the diagram at the end was effective, it could not offset this avoidable searching and reworking, warranting 8 points.

Relevant records: Rounds 2, 3, 4, 5, 6.

## Combined process and experience evaluation

The process evaluation is 50/60 and the learning experience score is 33/40; adding them gives a total of **83/100**.
