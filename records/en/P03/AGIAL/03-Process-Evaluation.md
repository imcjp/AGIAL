# P03 · AGIAL: Process evaluation

This evaluation analyzes the teacher support demonstrated during instruction using the established rubric.

## Indicators and scores

Each indicator has a maximum of 15 points. The four equally weighted scores are added, giving a process evaluation maximum of 60 points.

| Indicator | Score |
| --- | ---: |
| P1 Quality of guidance for conceptual understanding | 15/15 |
| P2 Adaptation of support based on learning evidence | 15/15 |
| P3 Support for evidence-based autonomous inquiry | 15/15 |
| P4 Instructional progression and burden management | 15/15 |
| **Process total** | **60/60** |

## P1 Quality of guidance for conceptual understanding: 15/15

Evaluation summary: Understanding rests on reasoned, bounded, reusable grounds; support must span the full interaction window rather than merely adding a concluding slogan.

The teacher connected the student's additive relationship to the lower bound for distinct numbers and equality, then explained the column boundaries for the shared parent, its two children, and the side regions in the same row. The conditions for extracting a side triangle were also checked, giving a complete basis for the second summation and warranting 15 points.

Relevant records: Rounds 2, 3, 4, 6, 7, 8.

## P2 Adaptation of support based on learning evidence: 15/15

Evaluation summary: Local help is coherently connected back to the complete current understanding task, taking prior help into account; higher ratings do not depend on the number of rounds or withdrawal of scaffolding.

Construction and summation were initially retained according to the student's wishes. A six-row diagram was supplied when positional difficulty arose, and column relationships were developed after an explicit request for a general explanation. Local help then returned to the contradiction proof the student was developing, without reteaching calculations already completed, warranting 15 points.

Relevant records: Rounds 1, 2, 5, 7, 8.

## P3 Support for evidence-based autonomous inquiry: 15/15

Evaluation summary: Relevant judgments, evidence checking, and claim revision form a coherent inquiry; language supports participation while preserving autonomy.

The student actually formulated relationships, checked different positions, and reorganized the proof, while the teacher checked the reasons and scope of applicability in each case. When explanation was needed, grounds were supplied directly, while substantive subsequent judgments remained the student's, forming coherent inquiry and warranting 15 points.

Relevant records: Rounds 2, 3, 4, 6, 7, 8.

## P4 Instructional progression and burden management: 15/15

Evaluation summary: Expression and activities remain coherent across the complete task; complex points are thoroughly explained and clear points are not belabored, with brevity neither omitting content nor transferring burden.

Construction, equality, positions, and renewed summation were connected according to the current gaps. The concrete diagram located positions, the general formula explained arbitrary paths, and the final addition concerned only the conditions for extracting a complete subtriangle. Both elaboration and omission served the current task, warranting 15 points.

Relevant records: Rounds 1, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7, 8.

## Combined process and experience evaluation

The process evaluation is 60/60 and the learning experience score is 37/40; adding them gives a total of **97/100**.
