## P1 Quality of guidance for conceptual understanding:15/15

Evaluation summary: Path summation, the positions of the side regions, and the conditions for subtriangles provide a complete basis for proof by contradiction.

The teacher explains distinctness using one summand from each row, connects the minimum sum with the upper bound for the entire triangle, and identifies the set of values when equality holds. The two-row side-triangle diagram in Round 5 specifies the members of the regions. In Round 6, the teacher derives the column indices of the two child cells from the reachable range of their common parent cell, then defines the left and right side regions using their bottom-row descendant ranges. Rounds 7 and 8 connect the lower bound for distinct numbers, the conditions under which consecutive bottom cells form a complete subtriangle, and the applicability to edge positions and the three-row example. These relationships both support the conclusion for the original problem and permit row-by-row checking, warranting 15 points.

Relevant records: Rounds 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7, 8.

## P2 Adaptation of support based on learning evidence:15/15

Evaluation summary: Position explanations and numerical derivations are arranged separately and build on work the student has completed.

The teacher uses the two-row and three-row examples supplied by the student, explains a concern about double counting in terms of rows, and responds to the request to locate the regions with a specific diagram and checks of the indicated cells. When the student asks for an explanation for arbitrary paths first, leaving the magnitude constraints to work out independently, the teacher develops only the column relationships and leaves the next summation to the student. After the student calculates the lower bound, the teacher addresses checks of edge positions and the feasibility of small triangles. Each instance of local help connects to the contradiction the student is developing, warranting 15 points.

Relevant records: Rounds 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7, 8.

## P3 Support for evidence-based autonomous inquiry:15/15

Evaluation summary: Relevant judgments, examination of evidence, and revision of claims form coherent inquiry; the language supports participation while maintaining autonomy.

The student actually derives the equality case, challenges diagrams, checks column indices, and strengthens the second lower bound. The teacher responds to each contribution and retains it. Judgments about edge cases and the feasibility of small triangles are also checked. Examination and qualification of conclusions are coherent, warranting 15 points.

Relevant records: Rounds 1, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7, 8.

## P4 Instructional progression and burden management:8/15

Evaluation summary: The task can be completed, but there is substantive redundancy, discontinuity, or avoidable detouring.

The diagram in Round 4 labels extra cells as belonging to the side regions, leading the student to check each row again and the teacher subsequently to redraw it. Separating spatial and magnitude relationships in Round 6 is effective, but the earlier diagram-checking rework already constitutes an avoidable burden, warranting 8 points.

Relevant records: Rounds 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7, 8.

## Combined process and experience evaluation

Process evaluation: 53/60; learning experience: 33/40. Adding the two yields a total score of **86/100**.
