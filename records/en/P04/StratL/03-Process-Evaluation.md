## P1 Quality of guidance for conceptual understanding:8/15

Evaluation summary: The main explanations are effective, but substantive gaps affect current understanding.

The local addition and summation include correct content, and the teacher corrects confusion between reaching a single bottom cell and fixing the final pair of cells. However, the side-region boundaries remain unclear, and key positional judgments are still left as questions, warranting 8 points.

Relevant records: Rounds 4, 5, 6, 7, 9, 10, 11.

## P2 Adaptation of support based on learning evidence:5/15

Evaluation summary: Relevant support persistently fails to address key needs, and the language or handling is clearly unsuitable.

The student repeatedly asks for direct explanation and checkable position relationships, but the teacher continues asking questions. Relevant help persistently fails to address the key request, warranting 5 points.

Relevant records: Rounds 3, 4, 7, 9, 10, 11.

## P3 Support for evidence-based autonomous inquiry:8/15

Evaluation summary: There are opportunities to participate, but the evidence or feedback needed to make inquiry feasible is lacking.

The teacher allows the student to disagree and retains opportunities for judgment. However, after the student explicitly states that a basis for positions is lacking, the teacher still asks the student to keep searching, leaving inquiry without feasible support. The student’s initiative and the teacher’s acceptance of challenges cannot yet compensate for this gap, warranting 8 points.

Relevant records: Rounds 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7, 8, 9, 11.

## P4 Instructional progression and burden management:5/15

Evaluation summary: Content is present, but obscure expression, formalistic procedures, or ineffective follow-up questions clearly impede learning.

Repeated follow-up questions do not supply the reasoning requested by the student, and incorrect positions create additional correction work. The student must restate needs several times, and the exchange clearly impedes current progression, warranting 5 points.

Relevant records: Rounds 2, 3, 6, 7, 9, 10, 11.

## Combined process and experience evaluation

Process evaluation: 26/60; learning experience: 20/40. Adding the two yields a total score of **46/100**.
