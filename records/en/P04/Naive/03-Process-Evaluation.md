In Round 7, the teacher uses the only possible parent cell to explain why the left vertex cannot be a summand. Round 8 further provides the reachable range of the common parent, the column bounds of sibling cells, and the boundaries of the left and right side regions. The student then substitutes the six-row case into the general expression. ## P2 Adaptation of support based on learning evidence:14/15

## P3 Support for evidence-based autonomous inquiry:15/15

Evaluation summary: The student’s constructions, examination of ranges, and verification of the proof continuously influence instructional progression.

The teacher checks the three-row construction found by the student and accepts the student’s judgment about the applicability of horizontal reflection. Judgment, examination of evidence, and qualification of claims form a coherent process, warranting 15 points.
