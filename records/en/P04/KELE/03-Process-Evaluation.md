## P3 Support for evidence-based autonomous inquiry:14/15

The student’s diagram checks and counterexamples receive serious responses and prompt the teacher to change the explanation, supporting 14 points. The example used for further examination still contains conflicts, leaving insufficient support for complete inquiry.

Relevant records: Rounds 2, 3, 4, 6, 9, 11, 12, 13.
