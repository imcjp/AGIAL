Refer to what actually happened to explain the help or shortcomings that most influenced your scores, and identify the one aspect you would most like to retain or change. Please remain consistent with your scores; if there were no obvious shortcomings, say so honestly rather than inventing any. Student's original response:

I liked trying a small triangle myself first, and the table stopped me from trying things haphazardly. The teacher was willing to hear where I thought things were wrong; I could point out errors in the diagram without having to pretend to understand.
