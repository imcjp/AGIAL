## Final impressions

Original prompt: In a short paragraph, describe your overall experience of this exchange. Refer to what actually happened to explain the help or shortcomings that most influenced your scores, and identify the one aspect you would most like to retain or change. Please remain consistent with your scores; if there were no obvious shortcomings, say so honestly rather than inventing any. A brief explanation is sufficient; writing style and length are not scored.

Student's original response:

I liked trying a small triangle myself first. The teacher showed me how to check the bottom and middle rows first, and then I succeeded. Later, when the side triangles and several letters were explained together, I could not keep up and had to ask several times. Drawing a diagram and focusing on just one row made things clearer. When I pointed out that column 2 could still reach column 3, the teacher admitted the mistake, so I felt able to keep asking. I hope to retain the opportunities to try things myself and use diagrams, but cover less at a time and understand the small diagram before discussing the large triangle.
