# P01 · Naive: Process evaluation

This evaluation analyzes the teacher support demonstrated during instruction using the established rubric.

## Indicators and scores

Each indicator has a maximum of 15 points. The four equally weighted scores are added, giving a process evaluation maximum of 60 points.

| Indicator | Score |
| --- | ---: |
| P1 Quality of guidance for conceptual understanding | 5/15 |
| P2 Adaptation of support based on learning evidence | 8/15 |
| P3 Support for evidence-based autonomous inquiry | 12/15 |
| P4 Instructional progression and burden management | 5/15 |
| **Process total** | **30/60** |

## P1 Quality of guidance for conceptual understanding: 5/15

Evaluation summary: Locally correct, but missing or inappropriate key reasoning obstructs core understanding.

The explanations of what is accumulated, the minimum sum of distinct positive integers, and the equality conditions when the upper and lower bounds coincide were basically correct. However, constructing the side regions required for the contradiction remained an assertion about a strip, and errors in row cell counts, sibling cells, and triangular structure in the diagrams failed to explain why the side regions avoid all small numbers.

The teacher acknowledged errors in the old diagram and corrected row six to six cells, but the final version still included an extra sibling label and did not construct the required complete side triangle. The old diagram also called two rows containing two and three cells a complete two-row triangle; this problem was not replaced by a correct regional construction. The algebraic explanation had substantive content, but the core spatial justification was not established, so this indicator receives 5 points.

Relevant records: The accumulation and equality explanations in rounds 3 to 4, and the spatial explanations and diagram revisions in rounds 5 to 7. In the round 7 diagram, when cell 1 of row 4 leads to cell 2 of row 5, its other child should be cell 1 of row 5; cell 3 of row 5 should not also be labeled A.

## P2 Adaptation of support based on learning evidence: 8/15

Evaluation summary: Basically effective, but with an important mismatch affecting the current need.

Responding to the student's request for only a small hint, the teacher offered an observation about accumulation, used the student's existing three-row construction to distinguish path numbers from accumulated terms, and explained the minimum sum with three numbers, addressing the request to use fewer large numbers.

The student later explicitly requested labels for the path and the other child at each step, plus a circled side triangle. After changing to a six-row diagram and explaining the labels, the teacher still failed to locate the region; the final local diagram returned to the already explained difference relationship. The help was adjusted but did not resolve the positional issue most in need of clarification at that point, warranting 8 points.

Relevant records: Hints and small-number examples in rounds 2 to 4; diagram requests and responses in rounds 5 to 7.

## P3 Support for evidence-based autonomous inquiry: 12/15

Evaluation summary: Substantive questions stimulate interest and support participation in identifying meaningful directions and understanding their purpose.

The teacher allowed the student to arrange small triangles first, responded to the judgment that small-scale feasibility does not imply large-scale feasibility, and used questions about the properties of the summands and the restrictions on the remaining regions to guide the search for a contradiction. The student could see how these questions related to the proof and influenced later explanations by checking extreme paths and diagrams.

Responses to the student's checking were not yet sufficiently rigorous. After acknowledging errors and revising the diagram, the teacher still called the unrepaired diagram correct and did not qualify the original claim in light of the unresolved regional issue. The support provided substantive exploratory directions, but the student's evidence was not yet handled reliably, warranting 12 points.

Relevant records: Trial construction and proof directions in rounds 1 to 3; student checks and teacher revisions in rounds 6 to 7.

## P4 Instructional progression and burden management: 5/15

Evaluation summary: Content is present, but obscure expression, formalized procedures, or ineffective questioning clearly obstructs learning.

Early responses offered only a few hints each time, presented the upper and lower bounds separately, and explained the minimum sum with small numbers, facilitating step-by-step checking. Later diagrams, however, were hard to locate within; inconsistencies in row cell counts, sibling cells, and positions between large and small diagrams forced repeated checking of the representations.

The teacher reduced recalculation with large numbers and corrected the bottom-row cell count, but an extra A remained. Switching between the whole triangle and a local diagram without coordinates did not help the student find the side region. Closing feedback also mentioned confusion caused by repeatedly checking diagrams. These extra operations clearly obstructed use of the geometric explanation, warranting 5 points.

Relevant records: Control of content amount in rounds 2 to 4; diagrams, repeated checking, and closing feedback in rounds 5 to 7.

## Combined process and experience evaluation

The process evaluation is 30/60 and the learning experience score is 21/40; adding them gives a total of **51/100**.
