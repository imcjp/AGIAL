This evaluation analyzes the teacher support demonstrated during teaching using the established rubric.

## P1 Quality of guidance for conceptual understanding: 15/15

Evaluation summary: The two step counts for the common parent and side-region descendants are explicitly distinguished, connecting spatial grounds to the numerical proof by contradiction.

The teacher first explained the distinction between one summand per row and partial sums along the path, then used concrete six-row positions to explain why side-region members must retain their complete set of child cells below. Round 7 separately counted steps from the parent to the penultimate row and from the current row to the bottom, deriving sibling-cell ranges and same-row side-region boundaries. The student then substituted a six-row example to check consistency with the general relationships; conditions for extracting a complete subtriangle were also explained. The objects, positions, and conditions required for the two summations could be checked against one another. The score is 15.

Relevant records: Rounds 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7.

## P2 Adaptation of support based on learning evidence: 14/15

Evaluation summary: Precise alignment is supported by specific learning evidence and actual interaction; the teacher changes or retains suitable assistance according to visible needs, without requiring additional rounds.

The teacher reused the student's row-by-row accounting, explained concrete six-row positions first, and then provided general relationships as requested. When the student requested fewer indices, the teacher removed extra symbols and explained again, providing effective assistance. However, the original step-count wording had impeded current understanding. The score is 14.

Relevant records: Rounds 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7.

## P3 Support for evidence-based autonomous inquiry: 15/15

Evaluation summary: Relevant judgments, examination of evidence, and revision of claims form a coherent inquiry; the language supports participation while maintaining autonomy.

The student judged partial sums, side-region membership, general justification, and the final lower bound, and actively checked step counts. The teacher distinguished the different evidence for path and sibling cells, explicitly corrected the affected part, and retained the student's calculations and conclusion. Inquiry was coherent. The score is 15.

Relevant records: Rounds 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7.

## P4 Instructional progression and burden management: 8/15

Evaluation summary: The task can be completed, but substantive redundancy, leaps, or avoidable detours remain.

Explaining positions and magnitudes in sequence helped the student follow, but the incorrect starting and ending rows in Round 6 required another step-count check, followed by a full restatement of column indices in Round 7. The repair was adequate, but the reworking remained an avoidable burden. The score is 8.

Relevant records: Rounds 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7.

## Combined process and experience evaluation

The process evaluation is 52/60 and the learning experience score is 34/40. Adding them gives a total of **86/100**.
