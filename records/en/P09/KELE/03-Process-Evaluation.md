## P1 Quality of guidance for conceptual understanding: 15/15

Evaluation summary: The descendant ranges of recorded cells justify separation from the side regions and support the final lower bound.

Round 6 clarified that each side of the four-row example consisted of a single cell, and used the total number of left and right bottom-row cells to show that edge positions still leave a sufficiently large side region. In Round 8, starting from the path parent's reachable column range, the teacher separately proved which column of the final pair lies among the bottom-row descendants of the left and right child cells, comparing this with the side triangles' coverage of only the outer bottom-row cells. Round 9 verified the student's linked argument connecting equality, spatial separation, and the second summation, while retaining the column formulas as proof grounds. The score is 15.

Relevant records: Rounds 3, 4, 5, 6, 7, 8, 9.

## P2 Adaptation of support based on learning evidence: 14/15

Evaluation summary: Precise alignment is supported by specific learning evidence and actual interaction; the teacher changes or retains suitable assistance according to visible needs, without requiring additional rounds.

As requested, the teacher used a four-row diagram to explain the summands and then expanded the left and right child-cell ranges in response to positional objections. Concrete feedback showed effective assistance; an incorrect small example and an initially omitted justification meant it was not consistently coherent. The score is 14.

Relevant records: Rounds 1, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7, 8, 9.

## P3 Support for evidence-based autonomous inquiry: 15/15

Evaluation summary: The student's checks of regions and justifications are adopted and used to form a complete argument.

The student checked the small triangle's membership, edge positions, and reasons for excluding sibling cells item by item. The teacher converted these checks into definitions of the two side regions and a descendant-interval explanation that could be examined item by item. The student completed the numerical lower bound and independently linked the final contradiction. The teacher verified the argument and distinguished explaining reasons with the column formulas from independently writing the proof without reference materials. The student's demands for evidence affected the expression and grounds of claims. The score is 15.

Relevant records: Rounds 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7, 8, 9.

## P4 Instructional progression and burden management: 8/15

Evaluation summary: The task can be completed, but substantive redundancy, leaps, or avoidable detours remain.

A support failure entering the teaching, an incorrect small example, and omitted range justifications caused interruptions and further questions. The later case-by-case explanation provided organization, but the additional burden across the complete observation window was clear. The score is 8.

Relevant records: Rounds 3, 4, 5, 6, 7, 8, 9.
