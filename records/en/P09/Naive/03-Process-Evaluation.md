# P09 · Naive: Process evaluation

This evaluation analyzes the teacher support demonstrated during teaching using the established rubric.

## Indicators and scores

Each indicator has a maximum of 15 points. The four indicators are added with equal weight, giving a maximum process evaluation score of 60 points.

| Indicator | Score |
| --- | ---: |
| P1 Quality of guidance for conceptual understanding | 14/15 |
| P2 Adaptation of support based on learning evidence | 14/15 |
| P3 Support for evidence-based autonomous inquiry | 14/15 |
| P4 Instructional progression and burden management | 8/15 |
| **Process total** | **50/60** |

## P1 Quality of guidance for conceptual understanding: 14/15

Evaluation summary: Connects relevant concepts, reasons, and conditions through actual interaction, clarifying key breaks in understanding.

Claims about endpoint positions, exhaustive treatment of small triangles, and region size successively required correction; the connection between the two summations and the side regions was ultimately explained. Interaction clarified the main breaks in reasoning, but related concepts changed repeatedly across representations. The score is 14.

Relevant records: Rounds 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7.

## P2 Adaptation of support based on learning evidence: 14/15

Evaluation summary: Precise alignment is supported by specific learning evidence and actual interaction; the teacher changes or retains suitable assistance according to visible needs, without requiring additional rounds.

The teacher switched to concrete examples and position checks at the student's request, and later explanations resolved some questions. Earlier examples and descriptions of regions created further problems, leaving overall coordination of assistance limited. The score is 14.

Relevant records: Rounds 2, 4, 5, 6, 7.

## P3 Support for evidence-based autonomous inquiry: 14/15

Evaluation summary: Invitations become evidence-based participation in actual judgment; the teacher openly and rigorously takes up contributions concerning questions, criteria, or evidence and allows them to influence progression.

The student's objections about endpoints, enumeration, and regions were taken up, and the evidence influenced later explanations. The grounds supplied for judgment required several repairs, so the complete process cannot yet be rated as coherent inquiry. The score is 14.

Relevant records: Rounds 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7.

## P4 Instructional progression and burden management: 8/15

Evaluation summary: The task can be completed, but substantive redundancy, leaps, or avoidable detours remain.

The student needed to revisit examples repeatedly, correct positions, and distinguish region row counts from cell counts. The final explanation was usable, but the exchange still contained clear avoidable reworking. The score is 8.

Relevant records: Rounds 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7.

## Combined process and experience evaluation

The process evaluation is 50/60 and the learning experience score is 30/40. Adding them gives a total of **80/100**.
