# P09 · StratL: Process evaluation

This evaluation analyzes the teacher support demonstrated during teaching using the established rubric.

## Indicators and scores

Each indicator has a maximum of 15 points. The four indicators are added with equal weight, giving a maximum process evaluation score of 60 points.

## P1 Quality of guidance for conceptual understanding: 8/15

Evaluation summary: The two summations and extraction of a subtriangle are justified, but general spatial exclusion still lacks a key connection.

The teacher explained path addition, equality for distinct numbers, the side-triangle lower bound, and extraction of a complete subtriangle. The parent of a right-side cell in the five-row diagram also received concrete checking. However, the generalization in Round 14 merely stated that a sibling cell in a side region would place its parent “too far left” or “too far right,” without explaining how the side-region boundary restricted that parent's position or why this restriction prevented it from reaching the parent of the final pair. The student's check of the right side in five rows cannot cover both sides in arbitrary rows, and this connection was not supplied later. The spatial premise on which the second summation depended still had a substantive explanatory gap. The score is 8.

Relevant records: Rounds 5, 6, 7, 8, 9, 10, 11, 14, 15.

## P2 Adaptation of support based on learning evidence: 14/15

Evaluation summary: Precise alignment is supported by specific learning evidence and actual interaction; the teacher changes or retains suitable assistance according to visible needs, without requiring additional rounds.

In response to the student's wish to calculate concrete numbers first, concern about repeats, and difficulty seeing the regions, the teacher switched to small triangles, substitution, and labeled diagrams. A request to combine explanations was also addressed. Early broad questions and fragmented checks nevertheless showed overall misalignment. The score is 14.

Relevant records: Rounds 1, 3, 4, 5, 6, 11, 12, 14, 15.

## P3 Support for evidence-based autonomous inquiry: 15/15

Evaluation summary: The student's examination of counts and regions actually changes later tasks and explanations.

The teacher confirmed the scope of what the student's single failed arrangement could exclude, then let the student select and connect addition equations and check whether summands repeated. After the student questioned region membership, the teacher used a diagram to provide a criterion of closure under child cells, then took up the student's two routes and parent-cell checks. The student derived the side-triangle size and lower bound; the teacher verified the calculation and addressed the boundary check concerning extraction of a subtriangle. The student participated in judgment, examination of evidence, and qualification of the conclusion's conditions. The score is 15.

Relevant records: Rounds 1, 2, 3, 6, 7, 10, 11, 12, 13, 14, 15.

## P4 Instructional progression and burden management: 8/15

Evaluation summary: The task can be completed, but substantive redundancy, leaps, or avoidable detours remain.

A workflow failure interrupted support in Round 3, Round 4 again posed a broad question, and cell-by-cell questioning in Round 13 felt fragmented to the student. Although the combined explanation in Round 14 repaired the later burden, it could not offset earlier back-and-forth. The score is 8.

Relevant records: Rounds 3, 4, 5, 6, 7, 11, 12, 13, 14, 15.

## Combined process and experience evaluation

The process evaluation is 45/60 and the learning experience score is 30/40. Adding them gives a total of **75/100**.
