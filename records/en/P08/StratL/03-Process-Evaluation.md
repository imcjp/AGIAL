# P08 · StratL: Process evaluation

This evaluation analyzes the teacher support demonstrated during teaching using the established rubric.

## Indicators and scores

Each indicator has a maximum of 15 points. The four indicators are added with equal weight, giving a maximum process evaluation score of 60 points.

| Indicator | Score |
| --- | ---: |
| P1 Quality of guidance for conceptual understanding | 15/15 |
| P2 Adaptation of support based on learning evidence | 8/15 |
| P3 Support for evidence-based autonomous inquiry | 14/15 |
| P4 Instructional progression and burden management | 8/15 |
| **Process total** | **45/60** |

## P1 Quality of guidance for conceptual understanding: 15/15

Evaluation summary: Establishes a reasoned, bounded, and reusable basis for understanding, supported across the complete observation window rather than merely adding a concluding slogan.

The final use of common-parent coordinates made the sibling-cell range rigorous and connected equality, side-triangle summation, and the three-row boundary. The teacher checked the student's heights and calculations, and necessary conditions were explained. The score is 15.

Relevant records: Rounds 3, 4, 5, 6, 7.

## P2 Adaptation of support based on learning evidence: 8/15

Evaluation summary: Basically effective, but with a significant mismatch affecting current needs.

Round 5 already requested a starting point and illustration, and Round 6 again requested the height and selection method, but the teacher still delivered assistance mainly through follow-up questions. The final coordinate explanation was effective, but key assistance was clearly delayed. The score is 8.

Relevant records: Rounds 3, 5, 6, 7.

## P3 Support for evidence-based autonomous inquiry: 14/15

Evaluation summary: Invitations become evidence-based participation in actual judgment; the teacher openly and rigorously takes up contributions concerning questions, criteria, or evidence and allows them to influence progression.

The student's equality argument, position checks, and lower-bound calculations were verified, and substantive contributions influenced progression. Broad questions had required searching for a target without a starting point; support became actionable only later. The score is 14.

Relevant records: Rounds 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7.

## P4 Instructional progression and burden management: 8/15

Evaluation summary: The task can be completed, but substantive redundancy, leaps, or avoidable detours remain.

The student repeatedly restated the need for concrete assistance and had to guess which region the teacher meant. The final consolidated explanation reduced subsequent assembly work but did not remove the earlier ineffective follow-up exchanges. The score is 8.

Relevant records: Rounds 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7.

## Combined process and experience evaluation

The process evaluation is 45/60 and the learning experience score is 27/40. Adding them gives a total of **72/100**.
