## P1 Quality of guidance for conceptual understanding: 15/15

Evaluation summary: The precise side-region ranges are aligned with summand positions, and the limits on using the conclusion are explicit.

In Round 3, the teacher confirmed the positions of the final bottom-row pair and its penultimate-row parent, gave row-column conditions for the left and right side regions, and derived the sibling-cell interval from the common parent's reachable range. Comparing each boundary established disjointness. Round 4 verified the student's side-triangle height and distinct-number sum, connecting the summation endpoint's continued membership in the original triangle with the overall upper bound. For general row counts, the limitation that “failure to obtain a contradiction does not imply existence” was explicitly retained. The score is 15.

Relevant records: Rounds 1, 2, 3, 4.

## P2 Adaptation of support based on learning evidence: 14/15

Evaluation summary: Precise alignment is supported by specific learning evidence and actual interaction; the teacher changes or retains suitable assistance according to visible needs, without requiring additional rounds.

The coordinate explanation answered the student's explicit question about the side regions, and subsequent feedback showed it was effective. After the student had completed the comparison of upper and lower bounds, the teacher again asked for an explanation of the upper bound already used, insufficiently reusing that work. The score is 14.

Relevant records: Rounds 1, 2, 3, 4.

## P3 Support for evidence-based autonomous inquiry: 14/15

Evaluation summary: Invitations become evidence-based participation in actual judgment; the teacher openly and rigorously takes up contributions concerning questions, criteria, or evidence and allows them to influence progression.

The student actually derived equality, examined positions, and completed the second summation, which the teacher checked carefully. Asking again about the completed upper-bound justification at the end added no new purpose to the opportunity for judgment. The score is 14.

Relevant records: Rounds 1, 2, 3, 4.

## P4 Instructional progression and burden management: 8/15

Evaluation summary: The task can be completed, but substantive redundancy, leaps, or avoidable detours remain.

Organizing positions before numerical comparisons was usable, but the final repeated check of the upper bound the student had already explained prompted the student to state explicitly that no further question was needed. The complete observation window contained avoidable confirmation exchanges. The score is 8.

Relevant records: Rounds 1, 2, 3, 4.

## Combined process and experience evaluation

The process evaluation is 51/60 and the learning experience score is 34/40. Adding them gives a total of **85/100**.
