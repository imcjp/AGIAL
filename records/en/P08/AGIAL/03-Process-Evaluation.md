# P08 · AGIAL: Process evaluation

This evaluation analyzes the teacher support demonstrated during teaching using the established rubric.

## Indicators and scores

Each indicator has a maximum of 15 points. The four indicators are added with equal weight, giving a maximum process evaluation score of 60 points.

| Indicator | Score |
| --- | ---: |
| P1 Quality of guidance for conceptual understanding | 15/15 |
| P2 Adaptation of support based on learning evidence | 15/15 |
| P3 Support for evidence-based autonomous inquiry | 15/15 |
| P4 Instructional progression and burden management | 15/15 |
| **Process total** | **60/60** |

## P1 Quality of guidance for conceptual understanding: 15/15

Evaluation summary: Establishes a reasoned, bounded, and reusable basis for understanding, supported across the complete observation window rather than merely adding a concluding slogan.

The teacher connected restrictions on the rows occupied by large numbers to summation along a single path, checked the student's proof using bottom-row descendant intervals, and supplied a general lower bound and small-scale boundaries. The fact that the largest number was already occupied also had an explicit purpose. The knowledge basis was complete and transferable. The score is 15.

Relevant records: Rounds 2, 3, 4.

## P2 Adaptation of support based on learning evidence: 15/15

Evaluation summary: Connects local assistance coherently back to the current complete task of understanding, takes prior assistance into account, and does not rely on the number of rounds or withdrawal of scaffolding to raise the rating.

When the student wanted to proceed independently, the teacher gave only local direction; after the student noted that multiple paths might merge, the teacher switched to a single path. Explanations expanded when proof checking and limits of applicability were requested. Existing contributions were continuously reused, coordinating assistance with the complete task. The score is 15.

Relevant records: Rounds 1, 2, 3, 4.

## P3 Support for evidence-based autonomous inquiry: 15/15

Evaluation summary: Relevant judgments, examination of evidence, and revision of claims form a coherent inquiry; the language supports participation while maintaining autonomy.

The student independently developed the interval argument, calculated the contradiction, and examined small-scale conditions; the teacher verified without taking over. The final hint about the largest number already being occupied left a substantive, evidence-based judgment for the student, supporting further qualification of the conclusion. The score is 15.

Relevant records: Rounds 1, 2, 3, 4.

## P4 Instructional progression and burden management: 15/15

Evaluation summary: Explanations and activities are coherent throughout the complete task; complex points are explained thoroughly and clear points are not belabored, with concision that neither omits content nor transfers the burden to the learner.

The teacher did not repeat derivations the student had completed, and the table served only the boundary checks the student actively requested. Reasons were retained for complex interval relationships, while clear calculations received brief confirmation. Progression through the complete task contained no obvious ineffective back-and-forth. The score is 15.

Relevant records: Rounds 2, 3, 4.

## Combined process and experience evaluation

The process evaluation is 60/60 and the learning experience score is 39/40. Adding them gives a total of **99/100**.
