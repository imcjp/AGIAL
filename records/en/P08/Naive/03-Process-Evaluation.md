## P1 Quality of guidance for conceptual understanding: 15/15

Evaluation summary: Equality along the path and summation in side regions connect into a proof by contradiction, distinguishing exclusion of positions from overall nonexistence.

The teacher gave path addition and column restrictions on the common parent, then verified the student's resulting side-region boundaries and lower bound of 2542680. Round 4 further listed the two side heights and corresponding lower bounds for three-row and four-row triangles according to the final pair's position. It clarified that every endpoint position yields a contradiction for 2018 rows, whereas small row counts may exclude only some positions. Concrete counterexamples helped qualify the general conclusion, establishing grounds for checking positions and row counts. The score is 15.

Relevant records: Rounds 1, 2, 3, 4.

## P2 Adaptation of support based on learning evidence: 14/15

Evaluation summary: Precise alignment is supported by specific learning evidence and actual interaction; the teacher changes or retains suitable assistance according to visible needs, without requiring additional rounds.

Local hints matched the student's request to derive independently, and the column ranges helped complete the spatial constraints and lower bound. However, the final check of small examples introduced a counting error requiring the student to correct it again. The match was supported by actual evidence, but overall coordination remained limited. The score is 14.

Relevant records: Rounds 1, 2, 3, 4.

## P3 Support for evidence-based autonomous inquiry: 15/15

Evaluation summary: Relevant judgments, examination of evidence, and revision of claims form a coherent inquiry; the language supports participation while maintaining autonomy.

The student actually developed side-region boundaries, calculated the lower bound, and used small-scale counterexamples to examine quantifiers. The teacher checked and revised the claims, clarifying the relationship between different positions and overall nonexistence. Judgment, examination, and qualification were coherent. The score is 15.

Relevant records: Rounds 1, 2, 3, 4.

## P4 Instructional progression and burden management: 8/15

Evaluation summary: The task can be completed, but substantive redundancy, leaps, or avoidable detours remain.

Early hints were brief and clearly purposeful, but the later incorrect small-row explanation forced the student to recalculate side-region heights and endpoint cases. Correction resolved the problem, but the reworking that had occurred must still be counted. The score is 8.

Relevant records: Rounds 1, 2, 3, 4.
