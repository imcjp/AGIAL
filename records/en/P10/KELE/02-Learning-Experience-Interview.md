Refer to what actually happened, explain the help or shortcomings that most influenced your scores, and identify the one thing you most want to retain or change. Student's original response:

What helped most was first being asked to follow the larger-child path: I could derive the summation relation from a small example myself and finally connect the reasons for nonexistence.
