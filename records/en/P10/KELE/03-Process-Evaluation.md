# P10 · KELE: Process evaluation

This evaluation analyzes the teacher support demonstrated during teaching using the established rubric.

## Indicators and scores

Each indicator is scored out of 15. The four equally weighted scores are added to give a process evaluation total out of 60.

## P1 Quality of guidance for conceptual understanding: 15/15

Evaluation summary: The coordinate relations supplied by the student are checked individually and connected to the small example and proof by contradiction.

In Round 5, the teacher clarifies that the last pair in the four-row example occupies bottom-row columns 2, 3, with the single cells 8, 9 taken on the two sides. In Round 7, the teacher checks the student's reachable parent range, sibling-cell columns, and treatment of empty ranges in the left and right side regions, comparing the non-intersection conditions individually before connecting them to the height of at least 1008 rows and the second sum. Substituting the four-row case into the general expressions gives exactly the two single side cells, aligning specific positions with the complete contradiction. The coordinate derivation is clearly attributed to the student, warranting 15 points.

Relevant records: Rounds 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7.

## P2 Adaptation of support based on learning evidence: 8/15

Evaluation summary: Basically effective, but with an important mismatch affecting current needs.

In Round 6, the student explicitly states that they can calculate the numerical lower bound and need side-region coordinates. The teacher nevertheless merely asserts the strip region and develops the numbers. The key positions are checked only after the student supplies them; this cannot count as timely provision of the requested help, warranting 8 points.

Relevant records: Rounds 1, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7.

## P3 Support for evidence-based autonomous inquiry: 14/15

Evaluation summary: Invitations become actual participation in reasoned judgment; the teacher openly and rigorously takes up contributions concerning questions, criteria, or evidence and lets them influence progression.

The teacher accepts the student's column correction and finally checks their complete positional argument in detail, supporting an actual contribution. In the middle, an explicit request for help does not receive the necessary basis, and the student undertakes the part they had hoped the teacher would explain, warranting 14 points.

Relevant records: Rounds 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7.

## P4 Instructional progression and burden management: 8/15

Evaluation summary: The task can be completed, but there is substantial redundancy, a leap, or an avoidable detour.

The small example following dense notation introduces another column error, and vague regions lead to further questioning; the student eventually supplies the coordinates. The final synthesis is effective, but the sequence still involves substantial rework, warranting 8 points.

Relevant records: Rounds 1, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7.

## Combined process and experience evaluation

The process evaluation is 45/60 and the learning experience score is 31/40. Adding the two gives a total of **76/100**.
