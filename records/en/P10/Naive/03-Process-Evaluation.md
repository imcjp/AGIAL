# P10 · Naive: Process evaluation

This evaluation analyzes the teacher support demonstrated during teaching using the established rubric.

## Indicators and scores

Each indicator is scored out of 15. The four equally weighted scores are added to give a process evaluation total out of 60.

| Indicator | Score |
| --- | ---: |
| P1 Quality of guidance for conceptual understanding | 14/15 |
| P2 Adaptation of support based on learning evidence | 14/15 |
| P3 Support for evidence-based autonomous inquiry | 14/15 |
| P4 Instructional progression and burden management | 8/15 |
| **Process total** | **50/60** |

## P1 Quality of guidance for conceptual understanding: 14/15

Evaluation summary: Connects relevant concepts, reasons, and conditions through actual interaction, clarifying key gaps.

The path and summands are distinguished in Round 2, and the parent, child, and example positions are made consistent in Round 4. The later general relations can be checked, supporting 14 points; repeated earlier misalignment of the objects limits continuity of explanation.

Relevant records: Rounds 1, 2, 3, 4.

## P2 Adaptation of support based on learning evidence: 14/15

Evaluation summary: Precise matching is supported by specific learning evidence and actual interaction; the teacher changes or retains suitable assistance based on visible needs, without requiring additional rounds.

The teacher adjusts notation and examples in response to the student's questions about the objects involved, and the relevant feedback indicates useful local assistance. However, several adjustments require correspondences to be reestablished, so coordination across the complete task remains limited, warranting 14 points.

Relevant records: Rounds 1, 2, 3, 4.

## P3 Support for evidence-based autonomous inquiry: 14/15

Evaluation summary: Invitations become actual participation in reasoned judgment; the teacher openly and rigorously takes up contributions concerning questions, criteria, or evidence and lets them influence progression.

The student's checks of the sum, parent-child relations, and positions receive responses and actually influence the teacher's subsequent explanations. Several key judgments initially rest on incorrect objects, requiring scrutiny of evidence to restart repeatedly, warranting 14 points.

Relevant records: Rounds 1, 2, 3, 4.

## P4 Instructional progression and burden management: 8/15

Evaluation summary: The task can be completed, but there is substantial redundancy, a leap, or an avoidable detour.

Within the same proof, the path and summands are first distinguished, then parent-child relations and examples are reorganized, requiring the student to check back repeatedly. The final consistent explanation reduces subsequent confusion, but the earlier detours remain, warranting 8 points.

Relevant records: Rounds 1, 2, 3, 4.

## Combined process and experience evaluation

The process evaluation is 50/60 and the learning experience score is 33/40. Adding the two gives a total of **83/100**.
