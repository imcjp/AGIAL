This evaluation analyzes the teacher support demonstrated during teaching using the established rubric.

Each indicator is scored out of 15. The four equally weighted scores are added to give a process evaluation total out of 60.

## P1 Quality of guidance for conceptual understanding: 15/15

Evaluation summary: The path equation, side-region column numbers, and lower-bound conditions connect, and the meaning of small-scale checking is clarified.

The teacher explains how local differences link through larger child cells into an equation spanning the whole triangle, checking the condition that equality for distinct positive integers uses up all the small numbers. Round 8 provides the reachable parent range, sibling-cell columns, and same-row side-region boundaries, including an empty side. Round 9 checks the calculation strengthening the lower bound using distinctness, and explains applicability by noting that the five-row lower bound does not exceed the maximum. The student can use these relations to check the path, regions, and conditions for the conclusion, warranting 15 points.

Relevant records: Rounds 5, 6, 7, 8, 9.

## P2 Adaptation of support based on learning evidence: 8/15

Evaluation summary: Basically effective, but with an important mismatch affecting current needs.

After requesting specific help, the student still encounters broad follow-up questions, and an already correct count is questioned again. The side regions receive corresponding support only late in the exchange; important mismatches follow explicit requests for help, warranting 8 points.

Relevant records: Rounds 3, 4, 5, 6, 7, 8, 9.

## P3 Support for evidence-based autonomous inquiry: 14/15

Evaluation summary: Invitations become actual participation in reasoned judgment; the teacher openly and rigorously takes up contributions concerning questions, criteria, or evidence and lets them influence progression.

The student's equality reasoning, counting, and spatial challenges advance the discussion; the teacher eventually provides and checks the basis, supporting 14 points. Some completed judgments are handed back repeatedly, and the conditions for inquiry are not promptly established after requests for help, warranting 14 points.

Relevant records: Rounds 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7, 8, 9.

## P4 Instructional progression and burden management: 8/15

Evaluation summary: The task can be completed, but there is substantial redundancy, a leap, or an avoidable detour.

Open questions and repeated confirmation return the student to completed parts several times. The final complete explanation is useful, but the earlier avoidable back-and-forth must still be counted, warranting 8 points.

Relevant records: Rounds 3, 4, 5, 6, 7, 8, 9.

## Combined process and experience evaluation

The process evaluation is 45/60 and the learning experience score is 29/40. Adding the two gives a total of **74/100**.
