# P10 · AGIAL: Process evaluation

This evaluation analyzes the teacher support demonstrated during teaching using the established rubric.

## Indicators and scores

Each indicator is scored out of 15. The four equally weighted scores are added to give a process evaluation total out of 60.

| Indicator | Score |
| --- | ---: |
| P1 Quality of guidance for conceptual understanding | 15/15 |
| P2 Adaptation of support based on learning evidence | 15/15 |
| P3 Support for evidence-based autonomous inquiry | 15/15 |
| P4 Instructional progression and burden management | 15/15 |
| **Process total** | **60/60** |

## P1 Quality of guidance for conceptual understanding: 15/15

Evaluation summary: Establishes a reasoned, bounded, reusable basis for understanding; support must span the complete observation window, rather than merely adding a concluding slogan.

The teacher moves from a local addition relation to distinctness and the equality case, then fully specifies the reachable range of the common parent and the boundaries of the side regions in the same row. For the second sum, the teacher also explains closure of the subtriangle and that consecutive integers serve only as a lower bound. The reasons and conditions are complete, warranting 15 points.

Relevant records: Rounds 1, 2, 3, 4, 5.

## P2 Adaptation of support based on learning evidence: 15/15

Evaluation summary: Coherently reconnects local assistance to the current task of achieving a complete understanding, taking prior assistance into account, without treating more rounds or withdrawal of scaffolding as an automatic improvement.

The local hints preserve the derivation the student wants to undertake, and the teacher directly develops the column-number explanation when a spatial explanation is explicitly requested. Subsequently, the teacher uses the student's lower-bound calculation and adds only the conditions required to reuse the relation. Each instance of assistance reconnects to the current task, warranting 15 points.

Relevant records: Rounds 1, 2, 3, 4, 5.

## P3 Support for evidence-based autonomous inquiry: 15/15

Evaluation summary: Relevant judgments, scrutiny of evidence, and revision of claims form a coherent inquiry; language supports participation while preserving autonomy.

The student actually derives the equality case, checks positions, compares coarse and refined lower bounds, and questions the conditions for the side triangle. The teacher checks these contributions individually without taking over the judgments. The respective roles of the student's contributions and the teacher's assistance are clear, and the conclusion is grounded in evidence, warranting 15 points.

Relevant records: Rounds 1, 2, 3, 4, 5.

## P4 Instructional progression and burden management: 15/15

Evaluation summary: Explanations and activities are coherent throughout the complete task; complex points are explained fully, while clear points are not belabored. Concision does not omit necessary content or transfer the burden.

Existing calculations are used directly, the complex spatial relation is explained in one account, and the final section adds only the complete-subtriangle condition and the meaning of the lower bound. There is no repeated confirmation of completed content, and necessary explanations are not left for the student to supply, warranting 15 points.

Relevant records: Rounds 2, 3, 4, 5.

## Combined process and experience evaluation

The process evaluation is 60/60 and the learning experience score is 37/40. Adding the two gives a total of **97/100**.
